How billing works

  • A tournament is reserved when you create it. If you delete it before any result has been entered, you get it back – trying things out is free.
  • Purchased tournaments are valid for 2 years from purchase.
  • A tournament can be edited for 1 year after it ends. After that it is archived: read-only, the results stay publicly visible. With a club or association licence there is no archiving while the licence is valid.
  • A running tournament is never blocked, even if your credit is used up.
  • Invoice made out to your club, without VAT (small business under Section 19 UStG); licences can also be paid by bank transfer. Just send a request via the contact form.
