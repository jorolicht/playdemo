1. Offline or cloud?

TourneyService runs entirely in your browser. You can work in two ways:

Offline (local)Cloud Cloud
Accountnot requiredsign in with your email address
Dataonly in this browser on this deviceon the server, accessible from anywhere
Costfree forever, all tournament featuresper tournament or licence, see Support & Pricing
In addition–tournament page on the internet, online registration, “My Tournament”, result entry via QR code, display board, several editors
To try it out, start the demo on the home page: a ready-made tournament with players, groups and a knockout stage. Everything you change there stays in your browser.

You can move a local tournament to the cloud at any time (“My Tournaments → Export tournament to cloud”). The Offline App (“Tools” menu) can be installed and also works without internet in the hall.

2. Home page and “My Tournaments”

  • Demo – a prepared tournament to try things out.
  • New Tournament – create a tournament with several competitions (section 3).
  • Quick Start – a single competition without a surrounding tournament, e.g. for club training.
  • Search & Find – published tournaments by name, date or organizer.

The “My Tournaments” menu shows your recently used tournaments – local in the browser, as a file or in the cloud – and offers:

  • Open/Archive – load a tournament file (JSON).
  • Export tournament to file – save the local or the opened cloud tournament as a file. If both exist, a dialog asks which one to save.
  • Export tournament to cloud – move the local tournament to the cloud (signed in).

3. Creating a tournament

Enter the name, the organizer (usually the club), the sport and the start and end date. For cloud tournaments, the contact person and venue appear on the tournament page, the venue also as a map. After saving you see the tournament overview:

  • ClickTT – import, update and export (section 11).
  • Copy – template for your next tournament: competitions with stages, start times, venue, contact and homepage texts are copied, players and results are not. You choose name and start date (suggestion: the same weekday one year later); all dates are moved. When you start a stage, its name and system are pre-filled from the template.
  • Edit / Delete – change the tournament details or delete the tournament.
  • Save/Leave – save and close the tournament; for local tournaments you can save a file at the same time.
  • The dark bar below shows where you are (tournament / competition / stage) and leads to Registration, Participants and Results.

4. Competitions

A tournament consists of competitions, e.g. “Men’s A Singles” or “Women’s Doubles”. Use “Add Competition” to create one:

  • Competition type: singles, doubles, mixed or team (teams of 2, 3, 4 or 6).
  • Start with date and time, TTR from/to as the playing class.
  • Online registration Cloud (singles only): period “Registration from/until”, “Registration closed” and the maximum participants (empty = unlimited; further registrations go on the waiting list). Registration must also be enabled in the administration (section 12).

Clicking a competition opens its overview with stages and participant list:

Finalize closes a competition once all matches have been played; results and placings are then fixed.

5. Players, doubles and teams

Under Registration you select a competition and enter the participants:

  • Add Participant – one at a time with name, club, TTR and year of birth.
  • CSV Upload – many players at once, e.g. from an Excel list; alternatively the ClickTT import.
  • The Active tick decides who plays in the next stage – e.g. for players who did not turn up.
  • Doubles: two registered players are paired into a doubles team.
  • Teams: teams consist of regular players; the line-up is set before each encounter. Each player can only be used in one doubles.

Under Participants you find all players of the tournament with club and TTR.

Players who registered online themselves Cloud appear in the list with the status “registered”. Whether they actually play is decided with the “Active” tick.

6. Stages and systems

A competition is played in stages, e.g. a group stage followed by a knockout final round. Use “Start Stage” to create the first one and “Start successor” for the next:

Each stage has five tabs: Administration, Draw, Input, Results and Scoresheet. In the administration you choose the system, the sets to win and the participants:

SystemUse
Groups (3 to 8 players, also mixed sizes)preliminary rounds with tables; the best advance
Knockoutfinal rounds with byes and a match for 3rd place
Round robinsmall fields, everyone plays everyone
Double round robinfirst and second leg with mirrored pairings and an overall table
Double eliminationwinners’ and losers’ bracket with a final (optionally with a reset match); one defeat does not eliminate a player
Swiss systemlarge fields in few rounds; players with equal scores meet (section 9)

Match sets: 2 (best of 3), 3 (best of 5) or 4 (best of 7). The scoring system defines the rules of the sport (set and match scoring, table tie-breakers). For teams you also choose the system of the encounter (e.g. Bundessystem, Swaythling Cup, Corbillon Cup, Braunschweig system, pair system).

“+ Add Dummy” fills a field with placeholders for testing. “Generate Draw” makes the draw.

7. The draw

The draw seeds players by TTR and avoids players from the same club meeting early. In the Draw tab you can adjust the result before the start:

  • Clicking two positions one after the other swaps them; alternatively enter new seeding positions and apply them with “SET”.
  • Players from the same club who would meet in the first round are highlighted in red.
  • “Start <stage>” starts the matches. The draw is locked from then on.

8. Entering results

  • Table: enter the table as soon as a match is called. Players see called matches in “My Tournament” Cloud.
  • Sets in short notation: the loser’s points, negative if player B wins the set. Examples: 5 = 11:5, -5 = 5:11, 10 = 12:10.
  • The green tick saves the match. Plausibility and sets to win are checked.
  • Result entry via QR code Cloud: every score sheet carries a QR code. Umpires or players enter the result on their smartphone; a flashing info button shows the tournament director incoming results to accept.
Several editors Cloud: several tournament directors can enter results at the same time. Results from the others appear immediately; if two people enter the same match, the last entry wins (with a notice). All entries are recorded in the change log (section 12).

9. Tables, knockout draw and further rounds

Depending on the system, the Results tab shows tables or the knockout draw:

  • Start successor: once all matches have been played, the next stage starts with the qualified players.
  • Swiss system: after each round, “Start Round n” draws the next round. The rules: no repeated pairings, players with equal scores meet, club protection, TTR as close as possible; the bye goes to the weakest player of the lowest score group, never twice to the same player.

10. Score sheets, certificates and result list

Score sheets

In the Scoresheet tab you print the sheets of a round (four per page) or individually.

Certificates

You create certificates from the result list or in the certificate tab of the last stage. With a custom logo Cloud it appears at the top of the certificate. Custom templates (WordPress pages) are selected in the administration; in them the placeholders #Tourney, #Competition, #Name, #Place and #Logo insert the values.

11. ClickTT

Via the ClickTT menu in the tournament overview:

  • Import – entry list from ClickTT (XML): competitions, clubs, players with TTR.
  • Update – apply the changes of a newer ClickTT file.
  • Export – create a result file for ClickTT; you select the competitions and stages.

13. Backup, export and archive

  • Local tournaments exist only in your browser. Back them up regularly via “My Tournaments → Export tournament to file”. Otherwise they are lost when the browser data is cleared.
  • Load a file via “Open/Archive”. An existing local tournament is replaced; a dialog first asks whether to save it.
  • Cloud tournaments Cloud can also be exported to a file. One year after the tournament ends they are archived: results stay publicly visible, but editing is no longer possible. With a club or association licence there is no archiving.

14. The tournament page Cloud

Every cloud tournament has its own public page that you can share:

  • Competitions & Schedule with start time, playing class and status; start times can be added to your calendar.
  • Register Now – online registration (section 15) while registration is open.
  • My Tournament – personal view for players (section 16).
  • Results and Info (display board) in the header, Location & Directions with a privacy-friendly map.

15. Registering online Cloud

  1. On the tournament page, click “Register Now” next to the competition.
  2. Enter last name, first name, club and TTR rating; year of birth, email and WhatsApp number are optional.
  3. The security check (captcha) runs automatically; then click “Register”.

After registering, a note from the tournament director appears, e.g. “Please report to the tournament desk at least 30 minutes before the competition starts”. Only the tournament director sees email address and WhatsApp number. Registration is possible for singles competitions within the set period.

Waiting list: If the competition has a maximum number of participants, the tournament page shows the available places. When it is fully booked, the button reads “Join waiting list”; after registering you see your position on the waiting list. If the tournament director removes a participant or raises the maximum, players on the waiting list move up automatically in order of registration (marked “Waiting list n” in the registration list).

Email: If you enter an email address, you receive a confirmation of your registration or waiting list position and another email when you move up.

16. “My Tournament” Cloud

On the tournament page, tap “My Tournament” and select your name – no account needed. The browser remembers your choice. It shows:

  • Next matches with opponent; a called match is at the top with “Called – table n”.
  • Results from your point of view, green for a win, red for a defeat.
  • My competitions with group standing or position in the knockout draw (e.g. “Next round: Semifinal”), for doubles with your partner.

The view refreshes itself every 30 seconds.

17. Account, credit and licences

  • Account: register and sign in via “Login” at the top right, also with a passkey.
  • Credit: each cloud tournament uses one tournament from your credit – reserved when you create it. If you delete it before any result has been entered, you get it back. Purchased tournaments are valid for 2 years.
  • Club and association licence: any number of tournaments without credit and without archiving while the licence is valid.
  • A running tournament is never blocked, even if your credit is used up.

Prices and packages: Support & Pricing. We are happy to answer questions via the contact form.
